Live scan of every tracked strike's CE/PE premium vs its opening print today. As of —
Open = High — the leg moved down off its opening print without first trading above it (sellers in control since the open; bearish for that leg). Open = Low — the leg moved up off its opening print without first trading below it (buyers in control since the open; bullish for that leg). Once registered, a strike stays on the list all day — even if the open later breaks. Pending = the open level hasn't been revisited yet. Filled = price has come back to (or through) the open — the classic re-entry trigger. "Open" comes from the exchange's own day OHLC. The bridge auto-reseeds itself just after 09:15 IST (and on every new session day); the manual Reseed from exchange button remains as a backup.
